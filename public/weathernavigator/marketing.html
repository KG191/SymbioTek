Accessibility Innovation

A world first in weather navigation.
Colour Vision Safe Mode.

8% of men have colour vision deficiency. Standard weather maps use red-green colour ramps that make precipitation, temperature, and route severity indistinguishable. WeatherNavigator is the first weather navigation app to offer a research-backed CVD-safe colour palette — designed using the principles of Jenny, B. & Kelso, N.V. (2007) "Color design for the Color Vision Impaired", Cartographic Perspectives 58, pp.61–67.

Standard palette

Clear • Mild • Moderate • Severe

Green and red appear nearly identical to people with deuteranopia or protanopia.

CVD-safe palette WORLD FIRST

Clear • Mild • Moderate • Severe

Blue → yellow → dark red. No green. Discriminable by lightness alone — works for all CVD types.

Applied across all weather layers, route severity, alert banners, precipitation radar, temperature maps, and timeline displays.
