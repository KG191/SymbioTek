Wendy for Android

Privacy, terms and support for Wendy Performance Coach.

These pages describe Android version 2.0 and its workplace coaching service, including internal testing. Availability of version 2.0 depends on your Google Play release track. They do not change the terms for Wendy on iOS.

Your information and choices

Wendy does not require a Wendy account. Saved discussions and plans stay in the app’s private storage; online coaching and optional speech also use external services. Read the Android privacy policy for data handling, retention and deletion options.

Free outcomes and Premium

Three completed and saved CALM outcomes are included free. Premium lets you continue coaching after that allowance. The monthly plan is A$1.99 in Australia, with local prices elsewhere. Google Play displays your applicable price and renewal terms before purchase. Service usage limits apply.

Read the Android terms, including cancellation and consumer rights.

Support and privacy enquiries

Email symbiotek@symbio-tek.com. Include “Wendy Android” and your app version. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or confidential workplace discussions.
